Catch v0.40 Teams Extraction Strengthening
- Teams OCR now uses screen-position-aware course extraction.
- Top app-bar lines are strongly preferred as course names.
- Handles:
  [001]生産システム実習基礎_R08 -> 生産システム実習基礎
  [001]2S-J_プログラミン... -> 2S-J_プログラミング
  OCR split across separate code/name lines.
- Course candidates are scored using position, course code, school-subject words, and class labels.
- Course-code lines can no longer be mistaken for assignment titles.
- Assignment title still prioritizes the text immediately above "期限".
- Deadline still prioritizes the date/time attached to "期限" and ignores "提出しました".
- Full-page confirmation from v0.39 is preserved.
